DESIGN PRINCIPLES
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ Wireframe Fidelity:
   - Minimal styling
   - Thin borders (1px gray)
   - Simple white backgrounds
   - No heavy shadows or effects
   - Keep element sizes relative to wireframe

✅ Responsive:
   - Stack on mobile
   - Side-by-side on desktop
   - Touch-friendly sizes

✅ RTL Arabic:
   - All text right-aligned
   - Text in Arabic
   - Proper RTL direction

✅ Minimalist:
   - No colors except gray/white
   - Clean spacing
   - Simple typography
